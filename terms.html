Installation and your desktop

Install only software you are authorized to use. Review the installer’s plan and installation guide before confirming changes. Depending on your selection, installation changes shell plugins, the bar, theme or lock-screen presentation and restarts the shell.

Keep backups of important configuration and local-only calendar data. Compatibility varies with Omarchy and dependency versions. Preview releases and automated checks do not guarantee correct operation in every desktop environment.

Accounts and synchronization

Connect only accounts you own or are authorized to access. You choose whether to grant provider access. Google sign-in happens in your browser; iCloud uses an app-specific password. Keep your credentials and computer secure.

Synchronization can read, create, edit and delete real events. Shared-calendar changes can affect other people. Check the chosen calendar, dates, time zone and action before saving or deleting. Network failures, provider changes and conflicting edits can delay or prevent sync; cached information can be out of date.

You may disconnect at any time. This removes local account credentials and cached calendar records, but does not delete remote events or revoke provider access. See retention and deletion for the separate controls.
