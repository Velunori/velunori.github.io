About this policy

This policy covers the Velunori desktop suite, particularly its optional calendar connections, and this website. Velunori is an independent community project for Omarchy. Accounts connect only when you choose to connect them.

The calendar runs on your computer. Velunori does not operate a server that receives your calendar events or account credentials. Its implementation is available in the calendar source repository.

Calendar data and permissions

Local calendars store the events you create. Connecting Google authorizes Velunori to discover calendars and read, create, edit and delete events where your account has permission. These permissions provide the calendar list, agenda and bidirectional synchronization.

The Google scopes are calendar.calendarlist.readonly for discovery and calendar.events for event access. Data processed includes calendar names, identifiers, colors and access roles; event titles, dates, times, time zones, recurrence information, locations, descriptions and synchronization metadata. Provider responses may contain additional event properties, processed locally where needed to preserve unrelated properties during edits.

iCloud connections use your Apple Account email and an app-specific password to access calendars through CalDAV for the same calendar functions. Velunori does not request your main Apple Account password.

Storage and security

Events and cloud cache are stored locally in SQLite. Google authorization/refresh tokens or iCloud credentials are stored in private account files. Google passwords are entered on Google’s website. Sign-in opens your system browser and uses a temporary callback on your own computer.

The default directory is ~/.local/share/velunori/clock, or $XDG_DATA_HOME/velunori/clock when configured. Velunori restricts data directories to your user and database/account files to owner read/write access. Credentials are not encrypted by Velunori; operating-system security, disk protection and your backups matter.

Calendar requests go directly from your computer to Google or Apple over HTTPS. Cached events remain when synchronization fails. Velunori does not intentionally include credentials in its application logs.

Use, sharing and Google Limited Use

Calendar data is used to provide the calendar features you choose. Velunori does not sell it, use it for advertising or profiling, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. The application does not automatically send calendar data to the maintainers or grant them access to your account.

Synchronization sends changes to your connected provider. Changes in shared calendars may be visible to others under the provider’s sharing settings. Google and Apple process these requests under their own policies.

Velunori follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, when using or transferring data received from Google APIs.

Retention, disconnection and deletion

Local events, cloud cache and account credentials persist between sessions until you remove them. The local calendar cache has no automatic expiry period.

  • Disconnect: open Calendars, select Disconnect and confirm. This deletes that account’s local credentials, calendar records and cached events. It preserves remote events and unrelated local calendars.
  • Revoke provider access: remove Velunori in Google Account connections or revoke the app-specific password in your Apple Account. Disconnecting in Velunori does not revoke provider authorization.
  • Delete a remote event: deleting a synchronized event in Velunori sends a deletion to its provider, subject to your permissions. This may affect other people sharing that calendar.
  • Remove all local calendar data: close the calendar/shell and delete the directory described above. This also deletes local-only events. Provider-held copies and your backups are managed separately.

Deleting records or files does not guarantee forensic erasure from storage, database journals or backups.

Website and other services

This website has no analytics, advertising trackers, external fonts or account system. The installer configurator stores selections in this tab’s session storage; clear the site’s browser storage to remove them. Selection links may also carry choices in URL query parameters.

GitHub Pages hosts this site and downloads. GitHub receives ordinary web requests, including network information such as your IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Linked providers, community sites and donation services have their own policies.

Other optional desktop features contact their own services. Weather requests, for example, send your configured location or search query to the weather/geocoding provider. They do not give that service access to your connected calendars. Downloads and dependency installation contact the relevant hosting services.

Questions and changes

Contact the Velunori maintainers through project support. Report suspected privacy/security vulnerabilities through the private security-reporting channel. Never publish passwords, tokens or private calendar contents. Maintainers cannot erase provider-held data or files on your computer for you.

Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date. Any new use of Google data requires the corresponding disclosure and consent before it begins.
